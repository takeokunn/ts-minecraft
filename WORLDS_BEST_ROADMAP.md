# ts-minecraft -> World's Best Minecraft Clone: Verified Gap Roadmap (2026-06-27)

## 1. Executive Summary

ts-minecraft is already a remarkably complete clone: a 1.18-style multi-noise terrain generator, 19 mob types with a real FSM + breeding/explosion/teleport AI, vanilla-accurate hunger/health/XP/durability/enchant math, working dimension travel (Nether/End portals), a flood-fill redstone sim, villages with grounded structures and trading, an off-thread greedy-meshing worker pool with LOD/frustum-culling, a robust IndexedDB persistence stack, and a surprisingly full HUD/settings/audio UX surface. The honest gap to "world's best" is not breadth — it is **depth and finish** along four themes. **Theme 1 — feel/juice fidelity**: real audio samples, screen damage feedback, head-bob, particle vocabulary, and missing sound cues are the cheapest, highest-perceived wins. **Theme 2 — systems that exist but are islands**: the EnderDragon boss FSM and dragon-death sequence are fully written but never wired; pistons track `extended` but never move blocks; weather has a deterministic-thunder bug — these are "almost shipped" code defects, not greenfield work. **Theme 3 — AI & content depth**: straight-line mob steering (no A*), player-only targeting, hitscan projectiles, round-robin biome-blind spawning, and missing potions/brewing cap the survival ceiling. **Theme 4 — renderer correctness & engineering integrity**: cross-chunk face culling and worker sub-region remeshing are missing/stubbed, fog never tracks render distance, and the architecture-enforcement story is partly theater (5 of 12 checks orphaned, the network package merged bypassing them, 99% coverage not on the verify path).

## 2. Per-Dimension Scorecard

| Dimension | Shipped maturity (1-5) | #Real gaps | Headline gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| World gen & terrain | 4 | 7 | Single-octave Perlin (no fBm) → smooth lobed terrain |
| Survival systems | 4 | 8 | No status-effect/potion system at all |
| Mobs, AI & combat | 3 | 9 | Straight-line steering, no A*/pathfinding |
| Nether/End/Redstone/Villages | 3 | 8 | EnderDragon boss FSM + death sequence are dead code |
| Rendering & perf (NFR) | 4 | 7 | No cross-chunk face culling → boundary overdraw + seam faces |
| Persistence/robustness/net (NFR) | 4 | 7 | Multiplayer is a stateless relay (no authority/persistence/chunk sync) |
| Game feel, UX & audio | 3 | 8 | All audio is single-tone oscillator synth (no samples) |
| Architecture & test integrity | 3 | 7 | 5/12 arch checks orphaned; 99% coverage off the verify path |

## 4. Quick Wins (best perceived "world's best" feel per unit effort)

1. **Wire pistons to move blocks** (`[L]`) — unlocks the entire visible redstone-build surface from a flag that's already computed.
2. **Screen damage feedback** (`[M]`) — the single biggest "I got hit" readability cue; reuses the shipped weather-overlay pattern.
3. **Missing sound cues** (`[L]`) — eat/pickup/level-up/fall/splash at event sites that already carry a position; the world instantly feels alive.
4. **Fog tracks render distance** (`[S]`) — kills un-fogged pop-in and horizon fog-out; change-gated on an already-tracked ref.
5. **Fix deterministic thunder** (`[S]`) + **crosshair blend mode + hit-marker** (`[S]`) — two trivially-cheap correctness/feel fixes.
6. **Halve melee damage** (`[M]`) — restores the entire weapon-tier progression curve with a pure-data edit.

## 5. Deliberately Deferred / Out-of-Scope

- **True signed −64..319 chunk model** — cross-cutting epic across storage indexing, meshing, physics, persistence; ship the deepened-band middle path instead.
- **Authoritative netcode + binary protocol + chunk streaming** — the relay is adequate as "cosmetic co-op"; full server authority is a multi-quarter architecture program; document the relay's limits first.
- **Full vanilla mob roster (~75) and ~60 biomes** — diminishing returns; add the high-leverage few (slime/phantom/iron golem/wolf; badlands/lush caves) rather than parity.
- **Boat/minecart rideable entities** (`item-type.ts:126` stub) and **elytra flight** (placeholder block, no `glide` mechanic) — depend on a mounting/flight system not yet present; track in the feature matrix so placeholders aren't mistaken for complete.
- **First-person viewmodel / attack animations** — no viewmodel infra exists; large art+rig effort for modest feel gain versus the cheaper juice above.
- **Lingering/splash potion variants, beacon, DataFixerUpper-grade migration chains** — follow-ons gated behind the base status-effect and migration frameworks; not blocking core play.
